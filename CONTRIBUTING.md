# Development workflow

Work in your own fork. For each milestone create a branch (`git switch -c milestone-01`), make a small change, test it, then use `git status`, `git add`, `git commit -m "Describe what changed"`, and `git push -u origin milestone-01`. Open a pull request into your own main branch, ask your mentor for review, then merge. Start the next branch from updated main. At least seven meaningful student commits are the goal; never manufacture history.

Use AI to explain errors, critique your designs and identify edge cases. Do not paste secrets into AI tools or submit code you cannot explain. Record three specific examples of AI assistance and your own decisions. Ask your mentor to review, not implement, features.
