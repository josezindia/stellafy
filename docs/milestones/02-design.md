# Milestone 2 — Requirements and design

Sketch homepage, catalogue, cart, checkout and analytics dashboard in Figma or on paper. For every page name what users see, what actions they take and what happens next. Consult the brand guidelines. Complete the three TODO criteria in `criteria.md` before building features. Ask someone to explain how they would test each criterion without additional context.

Before moving on: five measurable criteria with reasons and a design for every page. Commit and push.
